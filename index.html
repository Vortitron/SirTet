SirTet

A tetris for those who prefer emptying the dishwasher

Matching crockery in the dishwasher clumps into tetrominoes that float up the column, pausing at each cupboard. Turn each piece the right way round and push it in sideways. The first piece claims an empty cupboard for its shape; after that every piece must fit flush with no gaps (a green outline shows where it would land, red means it will not fit), and each shape gets just one cupboard. Misses, misfits and dawdlers come back as chipped plates at the bottom. Fly the I-shaped cutlery flat into the drawer on top to clear a row, and swipe a full cupboard shut for a bonus. Don't let the dishes reach the cupboards!

  • Tap the piece to turn it; tap left or right of it to move (hold to slide)
  • Swipe up for the next shelf, swipe down to go back one; swipe across a cupboard to shut it
  • ← → move, ↑ / X turn (Z the other way), Space next shelf, ↓ back a shelf, 1-6 shut a door, P pause
